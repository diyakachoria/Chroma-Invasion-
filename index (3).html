CHROMA

You are a dot. The floor is a patchwork of colored zones. When your color matches the zone beneath you, you're camouflaged — guards can't see you, even up close. When it doesn't match, you're exposed.

  • Move — WASD or Arrow Keys
  • Your color shifts on its own, at random — both the timing and the next color are unpredictable. Watch the bar next to the swatches: its length shows time left, and its color previews what you're about to become.
  • Guards patrol fixed routes. Their translucent cone is their vision. If you're exposed inside it, they'll spot you and give chase.
  • Caught while exposed = game over. Touching a guard while camouflaged is safe.
  • Reach the glowing exit tile to win.
